# RouteTill visual system

An original product identity for an inspectable purchase. Mode: Persuade on `/`, Operate on checkout, merchant, passport and lab; Read on documentation.

The scope brief authorizes the complete design. Work is code-led. Seven explored structures: assurance ledger, evidence workbench, purchase folio (selected), approval inbox, route map, digital vault, receipt index. The purchase folio puts a familiar transaction summary beside the applicable evidence. Its signature is one continuous vertical proof rail whose steps expose their actual verification level.

The selected third direction follows Impeccable seed reference `seed:4db1bcc4128b7d30`. Challengers were considered for clarity: video feed, encoded sleeve and manual metaphor declined; airport wayfinding contributes decisive hierarchy; monochrome product marketing contributes adjacent evidence, not brand motifs. User instructions to proceed take precedence over additional selection/approval rounds.

## Identity
Original SVG route mark: one entry path resolves into a receipt corner. Wordmark uses Manrope 750. No external brand marks. This is not a trademark clearance claim.

## States and motion
Meaningful colors always have visible labels. Demo labels remain present on screenshots and passports. Payment stage motion is a short opacity/translate transition; no perpetual animation. Reduced motion disables transitions and animated scrolling. Visible focus is immediate.
